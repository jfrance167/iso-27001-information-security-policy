# Optional Human-Subjects Checklist

The repository's completed experiment uses synthetic data. This checklist is
only for a future survey supervised under the applicable school, fair, and
institutional rules.

Before collection:

- Obtain advance approval from the science-fair or institutional review body.
- Obtain informed consent and, for minors, required parent/guardian permission
  and participant assent.
- Explain that participation is voluntary and may stop at any time.
- Do not collect names, contact details, demographics, IP addresses, sign-ins,
  or other identifiers.
- Configure any survey platform not to collect email addresses or login data.
- Ask only whether generic account categories exist and which arbitrary labels
  share the same unknown password.
- State repeatedly: never enter an actual password or recognizable variation.
- Define who may access results, where they are stored, and when they are
  deleted before starting.

During and after collection:

- Reject any response containing apparent credential material; do not inspect
  or test it.
- Export only anonymous category and reuse-label data.
- Analyze aggregates and suppress very small groups.
- Do not publish row-level human responses in this repository.
- Report limitations, nonresponse, sampling method, and approval requirements.

The template deliberately excludes demographic questions because combining
them can make otherwise anonymous responses identifiable.
