# Security and Responsible-Use Guidance

This project is a transparent educational simulation. It does not install
malware, capture sensors, manipulate a real display, inspect files, or transmit
data across a real air gap.

## Safe scope

- Use only the built-in synthetic `LAB` payload or other non-sensitive test data.
- Keep experiments inside systems and physical spaces you own or are explicitly
  authorized to test.
- Do not present simulation results as physical measurements.
- Do not adapt the code to hide real information in a display or other device.
- Do not record people, screens, cameras, or rooms without informed permission.

## Physical-test restrictions

If a teacher approves a physical extension:

1. Use synthetic visual patterns and a clearly identified receiver.
2. Obtain permission from the equipment owner and everyone who may be recorded.
3. Use a non-sensitive room and remove private information from every screen.
4. Do not install malware or bypass endpoint controls.
5. Do not use heat stress, fan overrides, ultrasonic signals, or other methods
   that could damage equipment or affect people and animals.
6. Stop if any device overheats, behaves unexpectedly, or captures unintended
   information.
7. Delete incidental recordings according to the approved experiment plan.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Follow the repository-level `SECURITY.md`. Never include credentials, private
recordings, personal data, or sensitive facility details in a report.
